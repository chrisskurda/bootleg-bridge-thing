# bootleg-composer-thing

## 0.1.0

First release: play bootleg Composer (0.5.0 or later) from the Car Thing.

- Pads: hitting near the top of a pad plays louder.
- Two octaves of keys that bend as a held finger slides sideways.
- Waves drawn with a finger, which become a looping sample and a riff in Composer.
- Record into the looping riff, select riffs, loop, play and stop.
- A Loop page for Composer's live looper on the Mac's input: record and overdub on the big button or the knob click,
  a ping-pong delay with tempo-synced times, feedback and mix, plus undo, clear and keep.
- Presets 1-4 pick the four pages.
- The knob bends held notes. With nothing held it sets the delay feedback on the Loop page, and scrubs the song
  elsewhere.
